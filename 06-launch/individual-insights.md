## Friction points

_The biggest technical or strategic challenge you faced while developing your product concept._

The biggest strategic challenge was choosing the right success metric. In M3 I measured success as the agreement rate, but my feature only shows plans the consumer can afford, so it could lower the number of agreements on purpose. The biggest technical challenge was the prototype: it crashed because of a single apostrophe in the code, so the Continue button did nothing until I found and fixed the line.

______

## Key learnings

_A few surprising discoveries or insights you gained from the course overall._

1. Define success as an outcome the consumer benefits from, not a count of sign-ups.
2. A guardrail can be tripped by the feature itself, because sending consumers with no fitting plan to an agent may raise agent contact.
3. A 60-day metric needs a 60-day follow-up, so the length of a test depends on the metric.
4. The AI output is only as good as the context I give it. Adding my own evidence and trade-offs turned generic suggestions into usable decisions.

______

## Aha! moment

_Your main "aha" moment during the project process._

The aha moment came while writing the experiment brief. I saw that the real goal is plans consumers keep, not plans consumers start, so I switched my primary metric to the share of new agreements with no missed payment within 60 days. That one change made my hypothesis, experiment, launch plan and success metrics all point at the same outcome.
