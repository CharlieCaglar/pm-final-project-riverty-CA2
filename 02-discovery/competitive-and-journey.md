# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** A consumer in financial difficulty who cannot pay the full balance at once and needs an instalment plan.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** Create an affordable instalment plan they understand and can actually keep.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** The consumer is unsure what they can afford, does not understand why income information is needed, and may leave the digital journey or contact an agent for help.
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** No external tools are supported by the research. The main workaround is calling an agent when the digital journey does not provide enough clarity.
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** 1. Start the digital instalment journey.
 2. Try to understand what amount is affordable.
 3. Guess a plan, stop the journey, or call an agent when something is unclear.
 4. Explain the situation again to the agent because the digital information is not available to them.
 5. Agree to a plan with the risk that it may not be sustainable.
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** The consumer cannot confidently understand what they can afford or what will happen next, and when they ask an agent for help they may need to repeat information already provided online.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** N-02 chose the shortest plan because it looked right but missed the second payment. N-04 preferred calling because a person could explain the cost. N-08 shows that agents have to ask again for information consumers already entered online.
- **Your journey map file name (e.g. journey-map.html):** The biggest gap is helping consumers choose a plan they can actually afford and keep, instead of guessing and needing an agent when something is unclear.
