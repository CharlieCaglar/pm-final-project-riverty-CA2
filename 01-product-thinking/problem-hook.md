# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which Riverty business-unit case are you using?:** BU Collection: A Fair Way Forward
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** If Riverty does nothing, consumers will continue to struggle with fragmented instalment journeys, leading to abandonment, unsustainable repayment plans and avoidable agent contacts, limiting Riverty's ability to grow effective digital self-service.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** A consumer who cannot pay the full balance tries to understand what they can realistically afford, becomes uncertain about income questions, multiple claims and the consequences of missing a payment, and ends up contacting an agent for help.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** Consumers who cannot pay their balance in full are struggling to confidently create sustainable instalment plans digitally, causing avoidable agent contact and putting both recovery performance and Riverty's self-service ambition at risk.
- **Value proposition, based on the initiative in your assigned Riverty case, what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** Create a clear digital instalment journey that helps consumers understand their situation, agree to a plan they can realistically keep, and seamlessly reach an agent when needed, improving sustainable recovery while reducing avoidable contact.

## Cold-read your own hook
- **Is the business risk high enough to justify a new initiative, a high-stakes threat, or a minor inconvenience?:** Yes. If consumers cannot find a sustainable instalment plan digitally, they may leave the journey or contact an agent. This increases manual work and can also impact recovery.
- **Is the moment of misery a systemic problem or just an edge case?:** It is a real problem in the instalment journey. Consumers can have difficulties understanding what they can afford, what information they need to provide, and what happens if they cannot keep the plan.
- **Does the value proposition actually remove the obstacle you identified, or is it a generic feature?:** Yes. We want to make the digital journey clear and simple, help consumers choose a plan they can actually keep, and provide an easy handover to an agent when they need help.
